The rhythm

Tonight's list becomes tomorrow's service

Every service on this page runs on the same clock. It is the only way we can grill to order at this volume.

1

Before 9:00 PM — you order

Online, by phone or by email. Catering and event bookings need 48 hours instead of one night.

2

9:30 PM — we marinate

Proteins are portioned against the list and go into the marinade overnight.

3

6:00 AM — charcoal is lit

Lunch orders are grilled and glazed first, dinner orders from mid-afternoon.

4

Your window — it arrives

Lunch 11:00 AM to 1:30 PM, or dinner 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. You get a text when the driver leaves.

Where we deliver

Two runs a day out of Ember Lane. Outside these zones, pickup is always available.

Zone A — Downtown core

Both windows, every day. Free delivery over $60, otherwise a flat $4.90.

  • Lunch 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM
  • Dinner 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Zone B — Inner suburbs

Both windows, minimum order $35. Flat $6.90 delivery, free over $80.

  • Lunch and dinner windows
  • Same-day cut-off applies

Zone C — Outer metro

Dinner window only, minimum order $60. Flat $9.90 delivery.

  • Dinner 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
  • Catering by arrangement
