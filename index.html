The glaze

Sweet, salty, and reduced by hand

Teriyaki is two words: teri, the shine, and yaki, the grill. Skip either one and it is just soy sauce on meat. We do both properly.

  • Four ingredients, no shortcuts. Naturally brewed soy, real mirin, sake and raw cane sugar — reduced for 90 minutes until it coats a spoon.
  • Brushed, not drowned. Three passes on the grill build a lacquer that shines instead of a puddle that steams.
  • No corn syrup, no thickeners. The body comes from reduction, which is why the glaze tastes of grain rather than sugar.
  • Gluten-free tamari on request. Same recipe, different soy, no extra charge.
