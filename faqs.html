Payment, changes and refunds

By Zelle, or in cash to the driver. When you place an order the site issues a payment code that looks like 1-26253. Scan the Zelle QR code on your confirmation screen, send the exact total, and type that code in the memo field. No card details are ever entered on this website.
It is how we match your Zelle payment to your food. Every order gets its own code built from the Julian date — 1-26253 means order number 1 on day 253 of 2026. Put it in the Zelle memo exactly as it appears on your confirmation screen.
  • The number before the dash is your order number for that day.
  • The five digits after it are the year and the day of the year.
  • Lost it? Reply to your confirmation email or call us and we will read it back to you.
A payment with no code still reaches us, it just takes longer to match — and we cannot start an order we have not matched.
