How payment works

  1. Place your order. Nothing is charged on this page.
  2. We hand you a payment code that looks like 1-26253.
  3. Scan the Zelle QR on the confirmation screen and send the exact total.
  4. Type the code in the Zelle memo field so we can match it to your food.

Prefer to pay the driver in cash? Choose that option in step 3 and skip all of this.

Good to know

  • Cut-off is 9:00 PM. After that, the earliest window is the following day.
  • Delivery is flat $4.90 to Main Side or Ops Side, free over $60.
  • Change or cancel free of charge until the cut-off — call (619) 730-8655.
  • Gluten-free tamari glaze on any dish, no extra charge. Say so in the notes.
  • Catering for ten or more? Ask for a quote instead.
